## Relationships

- **REL-001: NEEDS_CLARIFICATION.** An absolute weapons restriction and a pilot override of automatic controls may interact. The text does not establish that the weapons inhibit is an overridable automatic control, or state their precedence; a confirmed contradiction is not demonstrated.
  - SFMC-REQ-015: The SFMC shall never permit the pilot to fire a weapon at a friendly spacecraft.
  - SFMC-REQ-035: The pilot shall always be able to override automatic spacecraft controls.
- **REL-002: NO_CONFIRMED_CONFLICT.** Both statements specify lower update-rate bounds (2 and 10 Hz). A rate at or above both minima satisfies both on any overlapping field; different fields or modes do not establish a contradiction.
  - SFMC-REQ-002: The SFMC shall update the displayed spacecraft position at least once every 500 milliseconds while the spacecraft is in Manual Flight, Autopilot, or Combat mode.
  - SFMC-REQ-038: During normal operation, the SFMC shall update flight information displayed to the pilot at least 10 times per second.
- **REL-003: NEEDS_CLARIFICATION.** Automatic destination selection and automatic flight to a safe location describe related emergency-navigation behavior. Their destination/location definitions, activation conditions and handoff are not tied together explicitly. Both could coexist; clarification is needed rather than a confirmed contradiction.
  - SFMC-REQ-033: If the spacecraft is seriously damaged, the SFMC shall automatically determine the safest destination.
  - SFMC-REQ-034: The SFMC shall be capable of automatically flying the spacecraft to a safe location during an emergency.
